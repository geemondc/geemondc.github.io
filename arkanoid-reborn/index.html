How to Play

Drag anywhere on the playfield — the Vaus follows your finger's movement, so your finger never covers it. Tap (or tap LAUNCH / FIRE) to launch the ball, fire lasers, or release a caught ball. Mouse: move to steer, click to fire. Keys: ← → and SPACE.

Clear every breakable brick to finish the round. Silver bricks take 2 hits. Gold bricks never break.

Power capsules fall from broken bricks — catch one with the Vaus:

SSlow — the ball slows down
LLaser — tap to fire twin lasers
EEnlarge — a wide Vaus
CCatch — the ball sticks; tap to launch
BBreak — the exit door opens; slip out to the next round
DDisruption — the ball splits into three
PPlayer — an extra Vaus

Watch for wandering enemies — they deflect the ball but pop for 100 points. Extra Vaus at 20,000 points. Survive all the way to Round 33… and face DOH.
