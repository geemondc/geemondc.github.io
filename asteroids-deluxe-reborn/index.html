HOW TO PLAY

ROTATELeft / Right arrows, or the ◀ ▶ buttons
THRUSTUp arrow — real momentum drift, just like the arcade
FIREHold Space or FIRE — auto-fire, up to 4 shots on screen
SHIELDHold Shift or SHIELD — invincible inside the ring, but the shield drains while you hold it
SATELLITEKiller satellites split into 3 homing diamonds, then 2 hunting arrows each — blast them all
SAUCERSSaucers shoot across the screen edges at your true position — no safe hiding
BONUSExtra ship every 10,000 points
