HOW TO PLAY

The classic pub darts game, rebuilt in glorious colour — aim the hand, stop the meters, and ride the dart home. Fun for the whole family!

  1. Drag the hand to aim the dart anywhere on the board.
  2. Tap THROW and take your routine — a breath, a bounce, focus. Then the DIRECTION ball bounces wall to wall: stop it inside the glowing PERFECT zone for a dead-straight dart.
  3. Next the POWER ball runs 0–100%. Stop it in the TRUE band (50–60%) and the dart flies flat to your aim. Too soft, it drops. Too hard, it sails high.
  4. Ride the chase cam all the way into the board. What you stop is what you get — no luck, just timing.
  5. Every game has its own goal — count down to zero, close your numbers, hit the numbers in order, or land closest to the bullseye. Outscore your friend to win the match!
