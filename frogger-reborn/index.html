Frogger Extreme

Same five lanes, same river — but EXTREME: triple the traffic, triple the trouble. Weaving motorcycles, a speed-changing police cruiser, oil slicks, whirlpools and a beaver that dunks its own log. Get every frog home... if you can.

HOW TO PLAY — EXTREME

Swipe on the playfield, tap the big hop pads, or use arrow keys / WASD — one hop per move. Dodge everything. On the river you must ride logs and turtles — open water kills, and diving turtles (and the beaver's log!) will dunk you.

EXTREME TRAFFIC — 3× the hazards: packed car clusters, trucks, the fast race car and the bulldozer. New by level: a weaving motorcycle that strays across its lane (L2), a police cruiser that surges and eases in its gap (L3), a wide semi-trailer (L4), and oil slicks that slide you one extra hop (L5).

EXTREME RIVER: alligators cruise the lanes and one's mouth may wait in a home bay; a snake rides a log and another patrols the median. New: whirlpools drag you sideways toward their center (L6), and a beaver that dives and dunks its own log (L7). Land only in an empty home bay — and beat the draining TIME bar.

10 pts each new furthest forward hop · 200 per frog home + time bonus · 200 bonus fly · 200 escorting the lady frog home · 1,000 for all 5 bays · extra frog at 20,000.
