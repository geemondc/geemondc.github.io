Frogger Reborn

Five lanes of traffic, one river, five homes — get every frog across, just like 1981.

PLAY IT ANYWHERE

Point a phone camera at the big square on the cabinet — or just open this page on your phone. Same frog, same river.

HOW TO PLAY

Swipe on the playfield, tap the big hop pads, or use arrow keys / WASD — one hop per move.

Dodge the cars. On the river you must ride logs and turtles — open water kills. Turtles dive: when they sink, get off!

Land in an empty home bay. Watch for the alligator's mouth in a bay, hedges, snakes (later levels), and the draining TIME bar. Riding off the screen edge is fatal.

10 pts each new furthest forward hop · 200 per frog home + time bonus · 200 bonus fly · 200 escorting the lady frog home · 1,000 for all 5 bays · extra frog at 20,000.
