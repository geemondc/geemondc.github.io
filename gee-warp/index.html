You are sitting in a starfighter cockpit. In formation, drag to steer and hold your leader inside the bullseye for five seconds to warp. In the asteroid field, drag to dodge the rocks — hits crack the glass and drain your shield. Enemy raiders weave through the field: hold one inside the center scope for about two seconds to get a target lock, and the lock fires a homing burst that destroys it. Hold the FIRE button in the lower right to stream laser bolts; bolts alone only throw sparks off a raider. Raiders sometimes fire back. The radar pod on the left shows live contacts: green for your leader, red for raiders, gray for the nearest rocks. Tap the sound button for cockpit sound effects.
