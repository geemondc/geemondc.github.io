Konami’s 1983 tube warp shooter. Warp from Neptune to Earth — clear every wave, or the rim is your grave.

How to play

Your ship rides the rim of the warp tube and rotates a full 360°. Enemy fighters burst from the center vortex, spiral out into formation around the core, then dive at the rim — shoot them down before they reach you. Fight through the warps Neptune → Uranus → Saturn → Jupiter → Mars → Earth, then the whole run repeats, harder.

Watch for the satellite pod drifting mid-field: destroy the pod’s core to win DOUBLE FIRE — twin parallel shots that last until you die — and big points with it. Its two orbiting satellites can be picked off for bonus points. Meteors are indestructible — dodge them. On CHANCE STAGES the formations stream past for target practice: no enemy fire, no deaths, pure bonus points — destroy them all for the perfect bonus.
