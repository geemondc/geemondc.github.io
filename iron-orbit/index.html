📖 How to Play

The duel: best of 3 rounds. Chip your rival's health, break his guard, and go for the big finish.

  • 🪓 Limb loss is the tactic: hit his weapon arm clean and the arm comes OFF — blood everywhere. A disarmed knight can't strike… he runs!
  • Chase or escape: a knight with no arm flees to the teleport pads or the exit glyph. If he escapes, the round ends. Corner him and he can still shove and dash.
  • 💀 The big finish: a charged sweep to the core can take his head clean off. Round over, dramatically.
  • 🛡 Guard smart: blocking drains stamina. A block in the split-second before impact is a PERFECT BLOCK — slow-motion sparkle and a free counter window.
  • ✦ Arena toys: stand on a teleport pad to warp across. Momentum discs give a speed boost (with a show-off trail). Flying discs stagger whoever they clip. Stand on the golden exit glyph for 3 seconds to escape and end the round.
  • 🌙 Nightfall: when night falls, the duel clock pauses and zombies & vampires pour in. Both knights fight them TOGETHER — splashy, silly, lots of blood, never scary. Clear the wave and the duel resumes.

Blood is bright cartoon red and proud of it. Prefer sparks? Turn GORE off in Sound & Settings.
