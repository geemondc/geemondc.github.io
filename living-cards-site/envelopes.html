Living Photos vs. Living Cards

A Living Photo takes one of your pictures and brings it gently to life with light. The same photo travels from bright day, to golden sunset, to evening — the colors warm up, the lights come on, the mood changes. Nothing in the picture moves. It's your photo, just alive with light.

A Living Card is a little cartoon movie that arrives in an envelope. You tap it open, and inside, the characters actually do something — score the goal, break the finish-line tape, blow out the candles — in a six-second loop that plays over and over, ending on the happy moment with your message right there in the card.

One is a picture that glows. The other is a moment that happens.
