If you want to buy a Living Card, it's only a dollar each. Here are the envelopes you can choose from — and here are the cards you can choose from, that you personalize with your own words.

The more you get, the cheaper it is. One card is free — the Love card, back at the store.

One card = one card you make: pick the design, write your words, get your link. Share the link yourself, by text or email — anyone with it can open your card, as often as they like. A pack is cards you can make whenever you're ready. They never expire.
