How we do it — so you can, too

We don't keep secrets. A living photo is a photo you already love, with its light brought to life. Here's our whole recipe — take it and make your own.

It's old art tricks — color, light, and patience. Nothing fancy.

Watch one photo come alive

This is our corner of the world — one photo of home, three moments hiding inside it, then alive.

It starts as one photo — yours. Nothing added, nothing faked.

All we do is change the colors, the brightness and the shading. It's like magic — but it's not magic.

That's all we're doing — like flipping the pages of a little book: the same picture, its light changing a little each time, and it moves. Anyone can do it. But it takes time — you ask the AI, it makes mistakes, you do it again, and that costs money. Most people don't want to do all that. So we do it for them. That's the service.

After the Feast — one of ours, made with this very recipe.

1Start with a photo you already love.
2Never repaint the people. They stay exactly as they are — only the light moves.
3Ask the light to perform: day easing into night, a glow growing, reflections waking up. (An AI builder like Lovable does this part with you.)
4Make it loop — the end melts back into the beginning, so it can live on a screen forever.
5Always keep the original beside it. The difference is the whole point.

That's it. Go brighten a photo — and someone's day.

The Short Story

Who it's for

Anyone with a photo they love — the family, the trip, the night that mattered — sitting flat in a camera roll.

What it's for

Turning that one photo into a living page: its colors deepen, its light travels from day toward night, and it becomes a page you can open and share.

What's the problem?

A photo freezes one second and then just sits there. The colors you remember — the gold of that evening, the glow of that night — fade a little more every time you scroll past.

Can you fix it?

Usually, yes — if a photo has good light and color in it, there's a lot to bring out, and we do it by hand. Not every photo takes the treatment equally well.

How much does it cost?

Learning it is free — the recipe is on this page. Having us make yours: $5 for one photo — or $10 for three: buy two, get one free.
