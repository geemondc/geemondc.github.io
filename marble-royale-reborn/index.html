❓ HOW TO PLAY

🏁 The meet: 10 races. Everyone starts with $1,000 — biggest bank at the end wins. $3,000+ makes you Marble Champion! 👑

📋 The form: study each marble's last races and its odds. 🟢 SAFE favorites win often but pay little. 🔴 LONGSHOTS rarely win but pay big.

🎚️ Ladder bets: 1-1 bets cover half the marbles — 🔥 WARM vs ❄️ COOL colors, or 🌀 STRIPES vs ⚪ SOLIDS (striped marbles wear white bands!) — and pay ×2. 2-1, 3-1, 4-1 cover a set of marbles picked each race and pay ×3, ×4, ×5.

🛡️ Safe bets: ladder bets, ⭐ TOP 3, and PLACE & SHOW cash most often. 🎰 WIN pays the posted odds; QUINELLA, PERFECTA & TRIFECTA are the big gambles.

🎟️ Stakes: bets are in dollars — $5 steps, $50 max per ticket. Up to 2 bets per race. The $50 cap is the golden rule: nobody bets the farm!

🎟️ Across the board: the W/P/S buttons combine — WIN+PLACE+SHOW on one marble is 3 separate bets in 1 ticket. Your stake is per leg ($10/leg = $30 total — the $50 max covers the whole ticket), each leg pays at its own price, and the ticket shows its full math — every leg's price, the model's chances, and exactly what each finish pays — before you commit.

🏅 Results ceremony: after each race, the podium shows what every spot PAID, and the open race card lists every player's bets leg by leg, with the meet standings — the game keeps score in the open!

👀 Then just watch: roller-coaster courses — big climbs bunch the pack up, steep drops slingshot it apart, whoops bounce the order around. No controls — your picks do the racing!

🎢 Gravity plays favorites: Heavyweights bomb downhill, Sprinters climb best — so the lead keeps changing hands on the wild courses. Headwind fans punish the leader, turntables shuffle the pack, cannons fire marbles across gaps, funnels squeeze everyone through two-wide.

❓ Mystery gates: roll through a glowing gate, win a power — 🚀 ROCKET, 🧲 MAGNET, 🛡️ SHIELD (blocks the next hazard!), 🍌 BANANA PEEL (the next marble over it spins out!), ⚓ ANCHOR (bad luck — a heavy drag), ⭐ STAR DASH (untouchable + fast!).

🎯 The Seeker: on 🔴 WIPEOUT courses a homing spark-orb launches mid-race and hunts the LEADER — whistle rising — and bonks them back into the pack. Out front is never safe!

🚦 Course danger: every course is rated on the form — 🟢 CHILL (no hazards) · 🟡 WILD (some marbles get knocked OUT) · 🔴 WIPEOUT (marbles won't finish — every course's form shows its own measured average!). A wiped-out marble is DNF — it can't win, place, show or complete an exotic. Everyone else races on, and the winner is always a finisher!

💥 The hazards: THE GAP (jump it… or drop!), THE SWEEPER (a spinning arm that bats marbles off the track), THE TRAPDOOR (it flashes — then drops!), THE CRUSHER (don't be under it when it slams!), THE VORTEX (a swirl that swallows the tail of the pack) — and two courses hide a LOOP-DE-LOOP: too slow at the top and you FALL OUT. Heavyweights bulldoze through, the Steady Roller rarely gets caught, and the Wild Card flirts with disaster!

🪂 Catching air: kickers at the bottom of big drops LAUNCH marbles — they fly, shadow shrinking on the ground below, and can pass rivals mid-air! Clean landings bounce-and-go; rough ones wobble wide and drop spots. The ⚠ GAP JUMP breaks the track over a pit — carry enough speed to fly it (through a RING OF FIRE 🔥 on the wildest courses) or you're OUT. BIG AIR RIDGE is the extreme course: mega kicker, half-pipe, mud, smoke and a flaming gap jump!

🌋 Elemental chaos: 🔥 FIRE gates singe anyone who rolls through (fly over them instead!). 💨 SMOKE banks swallow the whole pack — who comes out first? 🌊 WATER crossings drag Heavyweights down while Sprinters skim across. 🟫 MUD pits bog the heavy marbles worst — Steady Rollers plow straight through. It's all telegraphed on the form, and it's all priced into the odds!

⚡ Surprises: every race hides ONE — boost rain, a wild spinning gate, a slick patch on wet tracks… plus live commentary, stumbles, surges and photo-finish slow-mo. Nobody knows when it strikes!
