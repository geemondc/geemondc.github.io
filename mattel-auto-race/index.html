How to race

Objective
Complete three laps. Each lap is one full drive from the bottom of the display to the top while dodging dimmer opponent cars. A hit beeps and knocks you down one row, but the race continues. Finish all three laps to stop the timer.

Controls
Tap START for the countdown and RESET to return to standby. Drag or tap the steering knob on touch screens; on desktop use ← →. Select gear 1–4: higher gears climb faster, but traffic comes quicker and is harder to dodge. Enter starts and R resets.

Driver ranking
Under 30s: World Champion Driver · 30–45s: Professional Driver · 45–55s: Showing Potential · 55–65s: Still an Amateur · 65–75s: Stick To The Highways · 75s or more: Leave Car In Garage.
