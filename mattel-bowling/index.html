How to play
  1. Move the top switch to 1 PLAYER or 2 PLAYERS.
  2. Tap either red side button to light the display.
  3. Click the SPEED display to cycle through speeds 1–7. Speed 7 is fastest.
  4. MOUSE: click and hold on the lane, move left or right to aim, then release to throw.
  5. KEYBOARD: use ← → to aim and SPACEBAR to throw.
  6. DURING ROLL: move the mouse or use ← → to curve the ball.
  7. In Touch mode, hold both side buttons and slide left or right to aim; release both to bowl.
  8. For a Bluetooth mouse, switch to Input: Mouse. Click and hold to aim, move left or right, then release to bowl.
  9. During the roll, drag or move across the lane to curve the ball.
