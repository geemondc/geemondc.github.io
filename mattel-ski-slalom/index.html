How to ski

Objective
Complete four downhill runs. Each run travels from the top of the display to the bottom while red LED trees and gates climb up the slope toward you. A collision beeps and pushes the skier back one level; there is no crash or game over. The timer runs continuously and stops after all four runs.

Controls
Tap START for the countdown and FINISH to return to standby. Drag or tap the steering knob on touch screens; on desktop use ← →. Choose SLALOM (speed 1), BRONZE (2), SILVER (3), or GOLD (4): higher events descend faster, but obstacles climb quicker and are harder to dodge. Enter starts and R resets.

Timer ranking
Under 30 seconds: Gold Medal Skier · 30–45 seconds: Silver Medal Skier · 45–55 seconds: Bronze Medal Skier · 55–65 seconds: Still learning · 65–75 seconds: Back to short skis · 75 seconds or more: Stay off the slopes!
