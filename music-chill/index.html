🎵 The Chill set

You gave me the genre — SiriusXM Chill, the Alex Lustig "Safe" sound: lush pads, soft piano, a slow beat underneath. Five tracks in that family. Press play on each, then just tell me which ones feel right — and for which room, if a room claims one.

1 — Voyage II – Satori · The Kyoto Connection
Soft piano with a long echo, built on Sakamoto's "Merry Christmas Mr. Lawrence" melody. Dreamy and spacious — the closest to "Safe" in this set.
2 — Something Elated · Broke For Free
Warm piano chords drifting over a laid-back beat. The classic chillhop cut — this is the Chill channel's home turf.
3 — Something Galactic · Broke For Free
Same artist, spacey side — dreamy pads, relaxed groove. Has "station lounge" written on it.
4 — The Calm Before The Storm · The Kyoto Connection
Mellow sax and piano over a gentle groove. Calm, open, unhurried.
5 — Wake Up · The Kyoto Connection
Piano and electronics on a slow journey from night into dawn. Soft all the way through.

All five are free Creative Commons tracks from the Internet Archive. The Kyoto Connection tracks are free for any use with a credit line. The Broke For Free tracks are free for personal use with credit — if one of those wins and a room ever goes on sale, I'll swap in a commercial-use twin of the same sound. Nothing here costs anything.
