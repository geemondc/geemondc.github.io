🎵 The Spa set

Third try, and this time the brief was strict: real spa music. No beat, no groove, nothing to nod your head to — massage-room stillness. Give each one twenty seconds; spa music doesn't announce itself, it settles. Tell me which ones calm the room, and which room each belongs in.

1 — Healing and Calm Meditation · Bulbasound
9:42. A slow soundscape made for massage and spa — soft atmosphere, no pulse at all.
2 — Deep Rest, part 1 · Anjey Satori
18:14 — the long one. Ocean surf under gentle synth, built for deep rest. Long enough to live under a scene without looping.
3 — Beautiful Piano Music with Rain · Bulbasound
8:18. Elegant piano over soft rain. This one has a room's name on it, I think — a rainy rooftop.
4 — Meditation 1 · Anjey Satori
8:48. Nature sounds and atmospheric synth, made for meditation and yoga. Peaceful all the way down.
5 — Inhale Exercise 1 · Solxis
4:25, from an album literally called Deep Spa Relax. Breathing music — slow in, slow out.

All five are free Creative Commons tracks from the Internet Archive, licensed for personal use with credit. If one becomes a room's permanent music and the rooms ever go on sale, I'll clear a commercial twin of the same sound first. Nothing here costs anything.
