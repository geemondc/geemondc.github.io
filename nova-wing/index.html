HOW TO FLY

STEER — touch and hold anywhere. Your ship turns toward your thumb: above center climbs, below dives, sides bank. The farther from center, the harder the turn — keep holding and she accelerates.

FIRE — hold the FIRE button (or mouse / spacebar). Line the crosshair up and let them have it.

TORPEDO — hold your crosshair on a fighter until it locks, then hit TORP. One shot, one kill.

RADAR — the scope at the bottom shows every fighter around you. Blips at the edge are behind you — swing around and hunt.

SURVIVE — enemy fire eats your shield first, then your hull. Clear the wave, earn shield back. Hull gone, run over.
