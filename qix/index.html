HOW TO PLAY
CONTROLS

Move: WASD / ARROW keys — or steer with the mouse, or drag on touch.

HOLD SPACE + steer to draw your line into open space. Mouse: hold LEFT CLICK. Touch: just drag.

Z = SLOW DRAW  ·  X = FAST DRAW — fast claims are worth more

Z = TRAZO LENTO  ·  X = TRAZO RÁPIDO — los reclamos rápidos valen más

P pause  ·  M mute

THE GAME

Draw lines to box in the Qix — the wild bouncing spark. Reach another edge and the smaller side is yours!

Fill 75% of the board to win the level. Every extra bit pays a 1,000-point bonus.

Watch out: the Qix pops your line, the red Sparx chase you, and the fuse burns if you stop mid-draw. Level 3: the Qix splits in two!

CONTROLS
CONTROL MODE

Every control is always live — keyboard, mouse and touch all work at once, in any mix. The switch above just remembers which style you reach for first; it never turns anything off.

KEYBOARD

Move: the arrow keys, or W up, S down, A left, D right.
Draw: HOLD SPACE + steer into open space.
Z = SLOW DRAW  ·  X = FAST DRAW (fast = double points).

MOUSE

Move: just move the mouse — the marker steers toward it.
Draw: press and hold the LEFT mouse button while you steer into open space.

TOUCH

Move: drag on the board.
Draw: just drag into open space — or tap SLOW (Z) / FAST (X) first to pick your draw speed.

P pauses the game. M turns the sound on and off.
