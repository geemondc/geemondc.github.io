The Robotrons have taken over. You are the last hope — destroy every hostile robot in the arena and rescue Mommy, Daddy and Mikey, the last human family. Inspired by his never-ending quest for progress, man created the Robotrons — and they have turned against their makers.

Insert coin. Press START.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Destroy all hostile robots in the arena to clear the wave and advance.
  • Rescue the family by touching them: 1,000 → 2,000 → 3,000 → 4,000 → 5,000 points per rescue in one wave without dying. The ladder resets when you lose a life.
  • HULKS CANNOT BE KILLED. Shots only knock them back a step. Keep away — they crush family members on contact.
  • Touching any robot, spark, shell, cruise missile or electrode costs a life.
  • Extra life every 25,000 points. You start with 3 lives.
  • Brain waves (5, 10, 15…) are thick with family — and Brains that hunt them and turn them into hostile Progs.

CONTROLS

  • Twin-stick (desktop): WASD = move · Arrow keys = fire direction · Mouse aim + hold to fire also works.
  • Twin-stick (touch): left thumbstick = move · right thumbstick = fire direction.
  • Simple (desktop): WASD or arrow keys = move · auto-fire at nearest robot.
  • Simple (touch): left thumbstick = move · auto-fire. Pause: P or the Pause button.
