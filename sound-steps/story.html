Why We Made This

Gary and his mom, Carol

My mom got new hearing aids. She was so happy. But the new sounds felt strange — too loud. Nobody showed her how to get used to them, a little at a time. The button was tiny and hard to use. After one month, the hearing aids sat in a drawer. My mom felt alone with this problem. She is not the only one. This happens to a lot of people.

Our backstory

My mom lived in Hollywood, Florida — our hometown. Then she slipped and fell, and she broke her hip. After that, she could not live alone. She needed assisted living.

I did not know that world. I had to learn it, fast. I visited place after place and asked a lot of questions. I wanted the best one I could find for her.

The best one was Sunscape, in Boca Raton. So I moved my mom up to Boca.

Getting used to a new home takes time — for her, and for me. It is like getting used to new hearing aids: small steps, one day at a time. That is where SoundSteps comes from.

Who is it for?

For anyone with new hearing aids. For moms and dads, grandmas and grandpas. For anyone whose new hearing aids feel strange, too loud, or too sharp. For families who want to help.

What is it for?

SoundSteps is practice for your ears. A few minutes a day. Soft tones and friendly words that get a tiny bit brighter each day — so new sounds stop feeling strange and start feeling normal.

1) What's the problem? New hearing aids change every sound at once. Nobody teaches your brain the new sounds. So the aids end up in a drawer. This is common: about 1 in 5 people who get hearing aids do not use them, and only about half wear them most of the time. The number one reason people give up is: ‘they don't help me enough.’

2) Can we fix it? We can't promise. Nobody can. But ears and brains learn with practice — hearing clinics know this, and good fittings even raise the sound little by little on purpose. If you put in a few minutes a day, your chance of success goes up. That is the honest truth.

3) How much does it cost? Zero. Nothing. SoundSteps is free.

New hearing aids are not like glasses. Your brain has to LEARN the new sounds. Learning takes practice. A little practice every day works better than one big change all at once. SoundSteps is that practice — the homework nobody gives you.

Your Log, Your Specialist

Every day you practice, SoundSteps writes it down. When you are ready, one tap sends your log to YOUR audiologist — any clinic, any town. You pick the email. Your log helps them tune your hearing aids to YOU.
