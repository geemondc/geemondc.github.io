HOW TO PLAY

Aliens climb the cosmic tube toward your Blaster on the rim. Shoot them down the lanes before they reach you — clear every alien to warp to the next well.

  • Flippers flip lane to lane — on the rim they flip straight at you. Contact kills. 150
  • Tankers split into two of what they carry when shot. 100
  • Spikers lay green spikes down their lane — shoot spikes away, and never hit one in the warp. 50
  • Fuseballs ride the lane edges and jump lanes — on the rim only the Zapper kills them. 250/500/750
  • Pulsars (level 17+) electrify their lane — be out of it when they pulse. 200
  • SUPERZAPPER: first press each level destroys all aliens on the well. A second press destroys one. It recharges every level.
  • Bonus Blaster every 20,000 points. Enemy fireballs can be shot down.
